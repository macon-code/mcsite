Macon Code MeetUp!

By the Community, for the Community!

We are Macon Code, a non-profit dedicated to solving community challenges through innovative technology. We believe in Macon’s potential and work to create resources that improve life for all.

Member-Driven Projects

We work on projects brainstormed by our members, encouraging creativity and collaboration to solve local challenges.

Skill-Based Networking

We connect members based on their unique skills, ensuring the right people work together to tackle the right problems.

Monthly Meetups

Our group meets once or twice a month, offering a great way to collaborate, share ideas, and make progress on community projects.

Open to All Skill Levels

Whether you're just starting out or an experienced professional, our group welcomes everyone. We believe that every perspective, from amateur to expert, contributes to solving community challenges.

Free to Join & Participate

Our group is completely free to join and take part in. We believe in open collaboration and making sure that cost is never a barrier to community-driven innovation.

Focused on Community Improvement

Our mission is to use technology for the betterment of the community. By working together, we aim to create lasting solutions that improve the quality of life for everyone in Macon.
